Thank you for listening!

[@icysamon](https://x.com/icysamon) is both my creative account and my personal, everyday account.  
I often use X's (formerly Twitter) "Following (Recent)" feed.

Since I use it every day, I have my own criteria for following back.

If any of the following apply to you, it's **quite likely** that I won't follow you back.  
These may be odd criteria, but I hope you'll understand.

- You frequently post AI-generated illustrations
- You make music by leaving everything to AI
- Your posts are all announcements with no sense of everyday life
- Your account is new, has been active only briefly, or has few posts
- You aren't involved in DTM (computer music production), game development, drawing (girls), electronics, or 3D modeling
- You frequently post political opinions
- You post discriminatory or prejudiced remarks

Even if none of the above apply, I may still not follow you back.

I will **definitely** follow you back if you post:

- Bishoujo illustrations
- Bishoujo music
- Bishoujo models
- Bishoujo robots (or electronics projects)
