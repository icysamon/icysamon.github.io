Thank you for listening!

[@icysamon](https://x.com/icysamon) is both my creative account and my personal, everyday account.  
I often use X's (formerly Twitter) "Following (Recent)" feed.

Because of that, I have my own criteria for following back.

In general, I follow back **creators** involved in fields like DTM (computer music production), game development, drawing (girls), and electronics.

However, I'm unable to follow you back if **any** of the following apply to you.  
These are just my personal criteria, but I hope you'll understand.

- You frequently post AI-generated illustrations
- You make music by leaving everything to AI
- Your posts show no sense of everyday life at all
- Your account is new or has been active only briefly
- You frequently post political opinions
- You post discriminatory or prejudiced remarks
